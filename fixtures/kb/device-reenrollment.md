# Re-enrol a replacement mobile device

When an employee replaces a managed phone, remove the retired device from the
device-management portal before enrolling the replacement. Ask the employee to
install the company portal application, sign in with their work account, and
complete the compliance check. The device must report **Compliant** before the
employee Wi-Fi profile and internal applications become available.

If the old phone was lost rather than replaced normally, escalate removal to
the security queue and do not ask the employee to approve prompts on that
device.
