# Recover MFA after a phone replacement

Verify the employee through the approved identity-verification procedure before
resetting MFA methods. Never request a password or a one-time code in a ticket.
After verification, revoke the retired authenticator registration and issue a
temporary registration pass. The pass expires after 60 minutes and may be used
only to register the replacement authenticator.

If identity verification cannot be completed, route the ticket to Identity and
Access Management without resetting MFA.
