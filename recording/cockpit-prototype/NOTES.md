# Prototype verdict

Question: how should the demo communicate that the AI drives the service-desk
workflow while a human retains meaningful control over the external write?

Provisional AFK verdict: use **A — Conversation first** for the main story. It
best makes Cora's intent, progress, citations, and request for approval legible
to product owners. Switch to native Freshservice for before/after proof, then
end on the actual AgentBus operator conversation for the technical audience.

Keep **B — Audit timeline** as a useful design reference for a compliance or
operations view. **C — Split cockpit** is information-dense and useful for live
operators, but it weakens the demo narrative because neither surface receives
enough attention.

The important product decision is not the visual skin: the AI may drive intake,
research, and proposal creation, but the exact approval and credential-bearing
write remain separate system responsibilities.
